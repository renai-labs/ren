The Ren mobile app runs on iOS and Android, and is in beta. Sign in with the same account as on the web.

| Tab       | What you can do                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Chat**  | Start a chat, answer Ren's questions and permission cards, and [connect apps](/docs/apps/#use-an-app-in-a-chat) from the connect card |
| **Tasks** | Read, start and review [tasks](/docs/tasks/), add comments and sub-tasks                                                              |
| **Drive** | Open the files of your [drives](/docs/drive/) and your [artifacts](/docs/artifacts/)                                                  |

In **Settings**, change the appearance, select **Check for updates**, or **Delete account**. Deleting your account works as on the web: see [Members](/docs/members/#leave-or-delete-your-account).
