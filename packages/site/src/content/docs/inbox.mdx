The **Inbox** of a [space](/docs/spaces/) lists its chats, newest activity first: the ones you started, the ones agents started from [automations](/docs/automations/) and [channels](/docs/channels/), and the chats of its [tasks](/docs/tasks/).

  shot="inbox/space"
  alt="The Inbox of the Engineering space with twelve chats from webhooks, schedules and people, their agent, who started them, where they came from and their last activity"
  caption="The Inbox of a space."
/>

| Column        | Shows                                               |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Chat          | Its title, and whether Ren is working or idle       |
| Started by    | The person who started it                           |
| Agent         | The [agent](/docs/agents/) that works in it, or Ren |
| From          | Where it started, such as Slack or a schedule       |
| Last activity | When Ren or a person last wrote in it               |

Select **Mine** to see only the chats you started. Select a chat to open it, or **New chat** to start one in this space.

Chats that wait for your answer or review also show under **Needs you** on the space's **Overview**.
