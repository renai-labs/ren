An app gives Ren tools for a service, such as Gmail, Linear or Shopify. To use an app, you connect an account of it, such as your Gmail sign-in, and choose the [spaces](/docs/spaces/) that use that account. Every chat in those spaces can then use the app.

## Apps and accounts

An app is the same for everyone in Ren. What you add to a space is an app account: the sign-in or key that Ren uses for that app. The account decides whose Gmail Ren reads, and which spaces can use it.

| Example                                   | App       | Account          | Spaces that use the account   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| You connect your own mailbox              | Gmail     | Maya's Gmail     | Personal                      |
| Your team shares one Linear workspace     | Linear    | Northwind Linear | Customer Success, Engineering |
| Ren pays for the service with its credits | Firecrawl | Managed by Ren   | Growth                        |

- One account can serve many spaces. Connect it one time, then add it to each space that needs it.
- One space can use more than one account of the same app, such as two Gmail mailboxes.

On an app's page, **Accounts** lists the accounts you can see. The pills next to each account show who can use it:

| Pill            | Who can use the account                                   |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal**    | Only you, in your personal space                          |
| A space name    | Everyone in that space                                    |
| **Every space** | Everyone in the organization. Only an admin can set this. |

A green dot means the account is connected. Other states are **Needs sign-in**, **Sign-in expired** and **Not connected**.

## Find an app

Open **Apps** in the sidebar. Search by name, or open a category, such as **Engineering** or **Sales and CRM**.

| Tab              | Shows                                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **All**          | The 350+ apps in Ren's registry, by category             |
| **Managed**      | Apps that can run with no sign-in, paid with Ren credits |
| **Organization** | Apps that your organization made                         |
| **Installed**    | Apps that one of your spaces uses                        |

Select an app to see example prompts, its accounts, its tools with their price, and its skills. Select a skill to read its instructions. Select an example prompt to start a chat with it.

## Add an app to a space

Adding an app to a space means choosing the account that the space uses.

1. On the app's page, select **Add to space**. When one of your spaces already uses the app, the button is **Open in chat**.
2. Under **Add to**, pick the space.
3. Pick the account that the space uses, then select **Add to** and the space name:
   - **Managed by Ren**, when the app offers it. There is nothing to connect.
   - An account that you already have. Everyone in the space can then use it.
   - **Connect a new account**: sign in in the window that opens.

### From a space

Open the space, then **Apps**. **Installed** lists the apps of the space, and **Custom** the apps that the space made. Select **Add app** to add one.

Select an app to see the accounts that this space uses. **Add account** connects a new account, or adds one that you already have.

### Managed by Ren

Some apps can run on Ren's own account. The space then shows **Connected · Managed by Ren**, nothing needs a sign-in, and each tool call uses [Ren credits](/docs/usage/). The app's page shows the price of each tool.

To use your own plan instead, pick your account for that space. Ren asks first, and names the space that stops using Ren credits. To go back, pick **Managed by Ren**: your accounts leave that space and stay in their other spaces.

### When you cannot add an account

Your personal account works only in your personal space. When you add it to a shared space, Ren asks first: **It stops being only yours.**

An account that someone else made can be added only by that person or an admin. Ren shows who can, and **Copy request** gives you a link to send them. The link opens the app with the request ready.

If the app's sign-in is not set up for your organization, Ren says so. Select **Copy message** and send it to an admin.

## Use an app in a chat

Type `@` in the [chat](/docs/chats/) composer and pick the app, such as "@Gmail summarize my unread email from today". Mention an app when more than one app can do the request.

When Ren needs an app that the space does not use, or an account that is not connected, it shows a connect card in the chat. Select **Connect**, pick or connect an account, and Ren continues. The connect card works in the web app and the mobile app.

## Remove an app or an account

| To                               | Do this                                                                           | Result                                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stop one space using an app      | Open the space, then **Apps**. In the app's `⋯`, select **Remove from** the space | The space loses the app. Its accounts stay connected for their other spaces. |
| Stop one space using one account | In the account's `⋯`, select **Remove from** the space                            | The account stays in its other spaces                                        |
| Sign an account out everywhere   | In the account's `⋯`, select **Delete**                                           | Every space that used it shows **Not connected**. You cannot undo this.      |

An account that no space uses stays in **Settings → Credentials** while it has a sign-in. Ren deletes it if it has none.

If another app in the space needs the app you remove, that app shows **Needs** and the name of the removed app.

## Create an app

Create an app when Ren's registry has no app for your service. An app that you create holds MCP servers, skills or both. MCP is the protocol that gives Ren the tools of a server. A skill is a set of instructions that Ren loads when a request needs it.

<Screenshot
  shot="apps/create"
  frame="detail"
  alt="The Create app dialog with the DeepWiki MCP server checked: No Auth found, 3 tools, and the name and description filled in"
  caption="Ren reads the server as you paste its address."
/>

1. Open **Apps**, then the **Organization** tab, and select **Create app**. You can also select **Create app** in a space's **Apps** page.
2. Paste the address of an MCP server, or the command that starts it. Ren reads an address and fills in the sign-in, name, description and logo. You can change each of them.
3. To add skills, drop their folders on the dialog, select **Choose folder**, or select **From GitHub** and paste the link to a skill folder.
4. Under **Add to**, pick the space that owns the app.
5. Select **Create app**. When the server needs a sign-in, the button is **Create and sign in**.

Ren adds the app to its space with a first account, and opens the app's page. A secret that you type in the dialog goes into that account. People who read or edit the app do not see it.

### MCP servers

| Kind   | What you paste                                                | Tools show                                       |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Remote | The address of a server, such as `https://mcp.acme.dev/mcp`   | When you paste the address, or after you sign in |
| Local  | A command that runs on the space's [machine](/docs/machines/) | After the first chat                             |

A remote server signs in with one of these:

| Sign-in      | What you give                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| No Auth      | Nothing. For public servers.                                       |
| OAuth 2.0    | A sign-in on the service's page                                    |
| Bearer Token | A token                                                            |
| API Key      | A key that Ren sends in a header or the query string that you name |

A local server's **Env** values are part of the app: everyone who can see the app can read them. Put a secret under **Secrets** instead. Each account asks for its value.

### Skills

A skill is a folder with a `SKILL.md` file and optional files, such as templates or scripts. Ren reads each skill's description in every chat, and loads the full instructions only when a request needs them.

| Use                                 | For                                                    | Ren reads it            |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| [Instructions](/docs/instructions/) | Rules for every chat, such as tone or which repository | On every turn           |
| A skill                             | A procedure that only some requests need               | When a request needs it |
| The chat prompt                     | A one-off request                                      | In that chat            |

If you paste the same steps again and again, make them a skill.

Ren checks each folder before it adds it. The name in `SKILL.md` must equal the folder name and use lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens, up to 64 characters. The description says when to use the skill, up to 1,024 characters. Every file must be text. For the full format, see [The `.ren` folder](/docs/developers/ren-folder/).

A skill from a folder is a copy: drop the folder again to change it. A skill from GitHub follows the branch, tag or commit in its link. A branch link picks up new commits with no edit of the app. Link to a commit to fix its content.

### Apps that need other apps

An app can include other apps of your organization, such as one sales app that includes your CRM app and a pricing skill. Adding it adds the apps it includes, and one account connects all of them. An included app can include others, up to 3 levels deep. Set this in **Spec**.

### Advanced and Spec

**Advanced** holds settings that most apps do not need: packages and build commands that Ren installs on the machine, env names for **Secrets**, and how long Ren waits for each MCP server.

**Spec** shows the whole app as YAML, and takes YAML or JSON. **Form** and **Spec** edit the same app. Webhooks are edited in **Spec** only. For the format, see [Ren as code](/docs/developers/ren-as-code/).

## Edit, change the owner or archive an app

The space that owns an app is its owner. You can see an app that your organization made, with its skills and their files, when you are a member of its owner space, a member of a space that uses it, or you have a personal account of it. Members of the owner space can change the app. On the app's page, open `⋯`:

- **Edit**: change the app and select **Save**.
- **Change owner**: make another of your spaces the owner.
- **Archive app**: every space that uses it loses its tools and skills, and its accounts stop working.

Changes reach each space in its next chat.

## When it fails

| What you see                                                    | Why                                                                 | Fix                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Couldn't reach the server. Check the URL, or create anyway.** | Ren could not read the address                                      | Check the address. You can create the app and fix it later with **Edit**. |
| **Tools show after you sign in.**                               | The server lists its tools only to a signed-in account              | Connect an account                                                        |
| **Couldn't add** and a folder name                              | The skill folder breaks a rule                                      | Fix each reason that Ren lists, and drop the folder again                 |
| **Sign-in expired**                                             | The service ended the sign-in                                       | Select **Reconnect**                                                      |
| Two apps have skills with the same name                         | Ren loads the skill of the app that came to your organization first | Rename one skill                                                          |
| Two skills have different names but similar instructions        | Ren picks one by its description                                    | Make each description say when to use that skill                          |
