A space holds the work of one team or one client: its chats, tasks, [Drive](/docs/drive/), [apps](/docs/apps/), [secrets](/docs/credentials/), [instructions](/docs/instructions/), [memory](/docs/memory/), [agents](/docs/agents/) and [machines](/docs/compute/). Only its members can see that work.

## Shared and personal spaces

| Space          | Who is in it                | Use it for                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Personal space | Only you. Everyone has one. | Your own work, and your Slack direct messages |
| Shared space   | The people you add          | A team or a client                            |

Every organization starts with **General Space**, a shared space for work the whole company shares. It stays the organization's first space after you rename it.

Create a space when work needs different people, apps or secrets. For example, an agency keeps one space per client, so only that client's team reaches its accounts.

## Create a space

<Steps>

1. Select **New space** in **Spaces**, or the `+` next to **Spaces** in the sidebar.
2. Type a name, and optionally a description.
3. Select **Create space**. You join it as its first member. Add others on its **Members** page.

</Steps>

## Find and pin spaces

**Spaces** lists the spaces of the organization: **Your spaces**, and **All spaces** for every shared space. To join one you are not in, see [Members](/docs/members/#space-members).

The sidebar shows your personal space and the spaces you pin. To pin a space, open `⋯` on it in **Spaces** and select **Pin**. You can pin up to 3.

## Overview

The **Overview** page of a space shows:

- **Needs you**: tasks that wait for review, tasks whose agent's last run failed, and chats that are running now.
- The number of chats, tasks done, pull requests merged and the cost, against the last period.
- **Recently done**: the tasks that finished.

A space with a [factory](/docs/factory/) shows the factory's week instead: what its agents shipped and what it cost, its activity, what needs you, and the time tasks spend in each stage.

## Settings

The space's **Settings** page changes its name, description, icon and **Default model**. New chats in the space start with that model.

To archive a space, select **Archive space** in its `⋯` in the sidebar or in **Spaces**. Its members lose access.

| Space               | Who can archive it      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| The first space     | Nobody                  |
| A shared space      | Any member of the space |
| Your personal space | Admins, not you         |

All members of a space have the same rights in it.
