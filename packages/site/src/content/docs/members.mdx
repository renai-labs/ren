Members are the people in your organization. Each member has a role in the organization, and works in the [spaces](/docs/spaces/) they belong to.

## Roles

| Role       | Can                                                                                            |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything an admin can, and change every member's role, including other owners                |
| **Admin**  | Manage members, settings, permissions, machines, usage and billing, and see every shared space |
| **Member** | Work in the spaces they belong to, and create spaces                                           |

## Invite people to the organization

Admins open **Settings → Members**.

- **By email:** select **Invite members**, type the emails, pick the **Invite role** and the spaces to add them to, then select **Send invite**. **Invitations** lists the ones not accepted yet, with **Resend** and **Revoke**.
- **By link:** on **Invite links**, create a link that expires **In 1 day**, **In 7 days** or **In 30 days**. Anyone who opens it joins as a member, and joins the spaces you picked. Share it in a team channel, and revoke it when you are done.

## Change a member

- To change a role, pick it in the member's **Role** column. Ren asks you to confirm.
- In the member's `⋯`, **Add to spaces…** gives them access to more spaces.
- **Remove member** in `⋯` takes away their access to the organization and its spaces.

## Space members

Open a space, then **Members**:

- **Add member** adds people of the organization to the space.
- **Invite link** creates a link for the space. Anyone in the organization who opens it joins the space.
- **Requests** lists people who asked to join. **Approve** or **Decline** each one.
- **Remove** takes someone out of the space. They lose access to its chats, apps and secrets.

To join a shared space you are not in, open it from **Spaces** and select **Ask to join**, with a note that says why. Admins can **Join** directly.

## Leave or delete your account

In **Settings → Preferences**:

- **Leave organization** removes you from it.
- **Delete account** signs you out everywhere. Ren deletes your account and its data within 7 days, and emails you when it is done.
