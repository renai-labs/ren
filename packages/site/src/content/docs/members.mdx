Members are the people in your organization. Each member has a role in the organization, and works in the [spaces](/docs/spaces/) they belong to.

## Roles

| Role       | Can                                                                                            |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything an admin can, and change every member's role, including other owners                |
| **Admin**  | Manage members, settings, permissions, machines, usage and billing, and see every shared space |
| **Member** | Work in the spaces they belong to, and create spaces                                           |

## Invite people to the organization

Admins invite people from **Settings → Members**:

- **Invite members** sends an email. The person joins the spaces you pick when they accept.
- **Invite links** makes a link to share. Anyone who opens it joins General Space and the spaces you pick.

## Change a member

- To change a role, pick it in the member's **Role** column. Ren asks you to confirm.
- In the member's `⋯`, **Add to spaces…** gives them access to more spaces.
- **Remove member** in `⋯` takes away their access to the organization and its spaces.

## Space members

Open **Space → Members**:

- **Add members** adds people from the organization. Invited people join when they accept.
- **Invite link** makes a link that adds anyone in the organization to the space.
- **Requests** lists people who asked to join, to approve or decline.
- **Remove** takes someone out of the space and its chats, apps and secrets.

To join a space you are not in, open it from **Spaces** and select **Ask to join**. Admins can select **Join**.

## Leave or delete your account

In **Settings → Preferences**:

- **Leave organization** removes you from it.
- **Delete account** signs you out everywhere. Ren deletes your account and its data within 7 days, and emails you when it is done.
