## Plan and credits

The page shows your current plan, the Ren credits it includes, and how many remain. Ren credits pay for model calls, the time machines run and paid tools. See where they went on [Usage](/docs/usage/).

## Change your plan

- **Upgrade** or **Subscribe**: checkout opens in a new tab. The new plan and its credits start once the payment clears, usually in under a minute.
- **Downgrade**: the lower price starts on your next billing date. Nothing is charged today, and your current credits stay.
- **Cancel plan**: you keep access until your next billing date, then move to **Free**. You can subscribe again at any time.

For plan prices and what each includes, see the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists each payment with its amount and status. Select one to open it.

If your organization pays a provider directly, such as a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, that provider bills you. See [Models](/docs/model-providers/).
