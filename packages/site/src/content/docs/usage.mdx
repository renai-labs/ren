Usage shows what Ren spent for you: model calls, the time machines ran, and paid tool calls.

## Organization usage

Admins open **Settings → Usage**. The page shows, for the period you pick (**Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**):

- **Cost**, split into **Models**, **Compute** and **Tool calls**, and the cost per day.
- **Chats**, **Model calls** with the share that failed, and **Tokens**.
- **Cost by** **Spaces**, **Members**, **Agents** and **Models**. Select a row to filter the whole page by it.
- **Credits and subscriptions**: your Ren credits and the model [providers](/docs/model-providers/) you connected.

To see one space, filter by it, or open the space and its **Usage** page.

## What costs money

| Cost       | Comes from                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Models     | Each model call of each chat, including subagents and chat titles                           |
| Compute    | The time a space's [machines](/docs/machines/) run                                          |
| Tool calls | Paid tools, such as apps that run on **Managed by Ren**. Each app shows its price per call. |

Model calls on your own provider subscription or key are paid to that provider, and show under its name in **Credits and subscriptions**.

## In a chat

The ring at the top of a chat shows how much of the model's context the chat uses. When it fills, send `/compact` to summarize the chat and free space, or start a new chat.

For plans and invoices, see [Billing](/docs/billing/).
