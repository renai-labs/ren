  Ren runs AI agents for your team. Send Ren work from the app, Slack, Linear, or a schedule. Then review what it did in
  one place.
</Hero>

## What Ren runs for you

<Spotlights>
  <Spotlight
    title="Projects"
    shot="projects/overview"
    alt="A project with its repositories, apps, and channels"
    href="/docs/concepts/projects/"
  >
    One kind of work, with its own instructions, repositories, apps, and storage.
  </Spotlight>
  <Spotlight title="Tasks" shot="tasks/kanban" alt="The Tasks board grouped by project" href="/docs/concepts/tasks/">
    One list for the work of people and Ren. Start Ren on a task when the task is ready.
  </Spotlight>
  <Spotlight
    title="Schedules"
    shot="schedules/list"
    alt="The list of schedules with their next runs"
    href="/docs/concepts/schedules/"
  >
    Run a prompt in a project every hour, day, or week.
  </Spotlight>
  <Spotlight
    title="Artifacts"
    shot="artifacts/grid"
    alt="A grid of dashboards and reports Ren built"
    href="/docs/concepts/artifacts/"
  >
    Dashboards and reports that Ren builds and keeps current at their own link.
  </Spotlight>
  <Spotlight title="Apps" shot="apps/marketplace" alt="The app marketplace" href="/docs/concepts/apps/">
    Connect the tools your team uses, such as Gmail, Linear, or Shopify.
  </Spotlight>
  <Spotlight title="Skills" shot="skills/marketplace" alt="The skills marketplace" href="/docs/concepts/skills/">
    Instructions that Ren loads only when a task needs them.
  </Spotlight>
</Spotlights>
