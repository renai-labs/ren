## How Ren is organized

<FactoryDiagram />

| Level                               | What it is                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Organization](/docs/organization/) | Your company. It holds members, billing and the settings every space shares.       |
| [Space](/docs/spaces/)              | Where a team or client works. Each person also gets a private space.               |
| [Agent](/docs/agents/)              | A specialized worker in a space, with its own model, instructions and permissions. |
| [Tasks](/docs/tasks/)               | Work that people and agents share, from backlog to review.                         |
