Sign up, connect Gmail, and get a summary of your unread email every weekday.

<Steps>

1. **Make an account.** Open the [sign-up page](https://useren.ai/signup). Select **Continue with Google**, **GitHub** or **Slack**, or use your email and type the 6-digit code that Ren sends you.

2. **Start a chat.** Select **New Chat**.

3. **Connect Gmail.** Send `Connect my Gmail account.` Select **Connect** on the card, sign in to Google, and allow access.

4. **Ask for a summary.**

   ```text
   Summarize my unread Gmail from the last day. Group it by what needs a reply.
   ```

5. **Put it on a schedule.** Send `Run this every weekday at 9am.` Open **Automations** to see the schedule and its next run.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Each run starts a new chat, unless you ask Ren to post in this chat.
- Ren does not know your time zone. Name it in your request, such as `every weekday at 9am Pacific`.
- A scheduled chat denies any action that needs your approval. To let the action run, set it to **Allow** in the space or agent [permissions](/docs/permissions/).
