Ren is made by Ren AI. You can delete your Ren account at any time, on the web or in the Ren mobile app.

## How to delete your account

**On the web**

1. Sign in at [useren.ai/app](https://useren.ai/app).
2. Open **Settings**, then **Preferences**.
3. Under **Account**, select **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm.

**In the mobile app**

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

If you are the only owner of an organization that has other members, make another member an owner first.

If you can't sign in, email [support@renai.build](mailto:support@renai.build) from the address on your account, and we'll delete it for you.

## What happens next

- You're signed out on every device, and you can't sign in again.
- We email you that we've received your request.
- We delete your account and its data within 7 days.
- We email you again when it's done.

## Data we delete

- Your profile: your name, email address and photo.
- Your sign-in methods, including linked Google, GitHub and Slack accounts.
- Your chats, files and memory.
- Your credentials and connected apps.
- Your memberships in organizations and spaces, and your preferences.

## Data we keep

- Invoices and payment records, for as long as the law requires us to keep them.

Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) to learn more about how we handle your data.
