# ren-v1 lessons

Evidence for [`../README.md`](../README.md). Each row is a rule for `Credentials` or for the app. Commits are in `../ren-v1`. The ids INV, DS and OI come from [`../../../ren-v1-hld/references/`](../../../ren-v1-hld/references/).

## Refresh and race bugs

| #   | What broke                                                                                                                                 | Fix                                                                                                                                               | Commit                                | Rule                                               |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Two refreshes at once used one single-use refresh token. The provider revoked the grant.                                                   | A 20 s lease on the credential row. The others wait for the winner's token. No DB connection is held during the HTTP call.                        | `81aa5bc0b`, `7de288d09`              | One refresh per credential at a time.              |
| 2   | Refresh ran on each manifest read. Many pods refreshed at once. A crash during a rotation left a spent token.                              | A single 5-min sweep, 15 min before expiry, no retry. Reads never refresh. A `token_expires_at` column, so the sweep does not decrypt.            | `03f127d65`                           | Refresh before expiry, never on the request path.  |
| 3   | A revoked grant was retried every 10 min and was never shown.                                                                              | A fatal error deletes the credential. The requirements check asks the user to connect again.                                                      | `03f127d65`, `da0c15fe2`              | Fatal means delete, and the UI shows "Reconnect".  |
| 4   | The MCP SDK reported GitHub's `bad_refresh_token` as `server_error`.                                                                       | Read the raw `error` from the response body.                                                                                                      | `ae318f543`                           | Classify on the provider's raw code.               |
| 5   | An Anthropic 503 with `invalid_request` in its body counted as a revoke.                                                                   | 5xx, 429 and network errors are transient. Only 400 or 401 with `invalid_grant` is fatal, and only if the credential's generation did not change. | `d8791547b`                           | A reconnect during a refresh survives.             |
| 6   | A failed ID-token check after an OpenAI refresh dropped the only valid refresh token.                                                      | Keep the rotated tokens. Check identity leniently on refresh.                                                                                     | `4e93b930d`                           | Save the rotated token before any other check.     |
| 7   | Refresh sent a `resource` that Ren derived. Klaviyo answered `invalid_target`; credentials were dead for 34 h; the sweep reported success. | Send the `resource` that sign-in sent. Count failures.                                                                                            | `f4e88b8cc`                           | Replay what sign-in sent (INV-87).                 |
| 8   | The token exchange and the refresh used different client authentication. Every public-client sign-in failed.                               | Public clients use `none`. Always send `client_id`.                                                                                               | `b65166b6f`, `8b04010fd`, `85eec4ec4` | One auth method from app to credential to refresh. |
| 9   | An org client secret could go to a token endpoint that the MCP server's discovery named.                                                   | Check at the one place where the secret is decrypted.                                                                                             | `5f2247e4d`                           | Guard where the secret becomes plaintext.          |
| 10  | A 30 s refresh window was shorter than the delivery gap.                                                                                   | 2 min, then 15 min.                                                                                                                               | `108d0be4b`, `6b8c27781`              | The window is at least the longest delivery gap.   |
| 11  | A new value in the environment did not reach a busy OpenCode. 401 until the turn ended (DS-T5).                                            | Not fixed in ren-v1.                                                                                                                              | —                                     | Injection at the egress removes it.                |
| 12  | A key saved over an OAuth credential dropped its refresh data.                                                                             | Reject a change of credential type.                                                                                                               | `bcad753df`                           | A type change is delete, then create.              |
| 13  | One sign-in `state` could be used twice.                                                                                                   | The first caller consumes it atomically.                                                                                                          | `cfda4236d`                           | One-time state.                                    |

| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |

| 14  | A copy of an OAuth credential would leave two live copies of a rotating token.              | OAuth credentials move; they are not copied.                                                                                        | `fa707d4ad`                           |
