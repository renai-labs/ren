## Working indicator, accessibility, i18n (notes folded into the rows above)

- Progress indicator: 16×16 animated PNG, 36 frames at 30 fps (1.2 s loop), clipped to a 5×5 dot grid, with a reduced-motion single dot (`v2/components/session-progress-indicator-v2.tsx`; test). Shimmer titles use `TextShimmer` whenever a tool or group is pending.
- Accessibility: `bdi dir=auto` everywhere; scroll regions are `role=region` with an aria-label; tool-loaded rows carry an aria-label with the visual parts aria-hidden; accordion `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`; TurnGap aria-hidden; reduced-motion CSS in basic-tool, markdown, count-label, status-title and progress indicator.
- i18n: all strings go through `useI18n` (`ui.sessionTimeline.notice.*`, `ui.messagePart.*`, `ui.tool.*`, `ui.message.*`, `ui.sessionTurn.retry.*`, `ui.sessionReview*`, `ui.toolErrorCard.*`), with plural forms (`i18n.plural`), `i18n.list`, and locale `Intl` number, date and compact formats. Marker tokens (`__OPENCODE_LOADED_SKILL__`/`_PATH__`) split translated sentences around styled values.
- MessageNav diff bars: 5 bars; fewer than 5 changes gives one bar per side; otherwise proportional (4 or 5 colored bars) (`components/message-nav.tsx`).
